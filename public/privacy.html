Privacy

This club site keeps only what it needs to know who’s playing.

  • From Google: your UCLA email address and Google’s ID for your account. Never your password.
  • Your name: the roster or guest name you sign in as, linked to that email so nobody else can use it.
  • Games: games you host or join are saved with your name to the club’s reservation sheet and shown to signed-in players.
  • Staying signed in: a cookie keeps this browser signed in for 90 days, renewed when you visit. Signing out removes it.
  • Officers: club officers can see which email goes with each name, to fix mix-ups and stop misuse.
  • Logs: server logs are deleted within a week.

Nothing is sold or shared, and there are no ads or trackers. To remove your account link, ask a club officer.
